Privacy, in plain words.

Last updated: July 2026

The short version

ColorSLCT runs entirely in your browser. There is no ColorSLCT server, no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising pixels and no third-party cookies. We cannot see your palettes, your prompts, or you — the architecture makes it impossible, not just impolite.

What’s stored on your device

When you first visit, a banner asks whether ColorSLCT may save your work in your browser’s local storage. Two answers:

  • Allow saving — your current palette, saved library, theme, preferences and tour progress are kept in this browser so they’re there next time.
  • Only what’s essential — just two things persist: your storage choice itself (so we stop asking) and, if you activate one, your license. Everything else lasts only until the tab closes.

If you tick “remember my key on this device” in the AI assistant, your DeepSeek API key is also stored locally — never anywhere else. You can change your storage choice any time via Storage choices in the footer, and erase everything via Preferences → Erase everything saved here in the studio.

The only network calls, ever

Three features talk to the outside world, each only when you use it:

  • AI assistant — your prompt, your current five colors and your own API key are sent directly from your browser to DeepSeek (their privacy policy applies). Skip the feature and nothing is sent.
  • License verification — if you activate a Gumroad license key, that key is sent to Gumroad’s verification API to confirm the purchase. Signed ColorSLCT keys are verified on your device with cryptography; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Buying — the buy buttons open Stripe or Gumroad checkout pages. Payment happens on their sites, under their policies; we never see card numbers.

Payments

Checkout is handled by Stripe and/or Gumroad. They process your payment details and email under their own privacy policies. What we receive from them is what any seller receives: order details and the email needed to deliver your license.

Your controls

  • See everything: your browser’s developer tools list every stored value under keys starting with cslct..
  • Delete everything: Studio → Preferences → “Erase everything saved here”, or clear this site’s data in your browser settings.
  • Change your mind: footer → “Storage choices” re-opens the consent banner.

Because nothing about you is collected or transmitted to us, there is nothing for us to sell, share, leak or be subpoenaed for. That’s the whole policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the contact details on your purchase receipt, or reach BNDR LLC via bndrllc.com.
