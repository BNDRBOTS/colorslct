06 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Asked, answered.

Is the free version actually usable?+

Yes — the whole studio: generation, WCAG checking and auto-fix, live previews, compare, history, CSS export and the AI assistant. Pro adds the power-tool layer: the full export pack, shade scales, image extraction, color-blindness preview and unlimited saves.

What does “one-time” mean exactly?+

You pay once and the license is yours, including every 1.x update. No renewal, no subscription, no feature hostages later.

Where is my data stored?+

In your browser, on your device — and only after you allow saving. There is no server and no account. Clearing your browser data clears your palettes, so export anything precious.

How does the AI assistant work?+

You bring your own DeepSeek API key. Your prompt goes straight from your browser to DeepSeek — never through us, because there is no “us” server to pass through. The key is kept only if you tick “remember on this device.”

How do I get my license after buying?+

Buying through Gumroad shows a license key on your receipt instantly — paste it on the activation page. Buying by card (Stripe) delivers your key by email. Activation takes one paste.

Does it work offline?+

Yes. Once the page is open (or if you own the files), everything except the optional AI call works with no connection at all.
