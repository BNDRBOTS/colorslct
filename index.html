Features

A serious color engine wearing a friendly face

Everything runs on real perceptual color math — the kind design systems teams use — wrapped in controls anyone can operate.

True OKLCH designer engine

The engine spins harmony models — complementary, triadic, golden-angle — into role-fitted palettes with gamut mapping and contrast math. Six moods and real color blending, not presets.

WCAG auto-enforcement

Flip one switch and every palette is nudged until text hits 7:1 and UI colors hit 4.5:1 contrast. Accessibility, handled while you play.

Live light + dark previews

See your colors on realistic light and dark interfaces — buttons, text, secondary labels — before they ever touch your product.

Compare & history

Pin up to four swatches side by side, drag to reorder, and step back through your last five palettes any time. Nothing is ever lost by accident.

Exports for real codebases

CSS variables free, forever. Pro adds SCSS, Tailwind config, W3C design-token JSON, and an SVG palette card — copy or download as files.

AI suggestions, your key

Describe a vibe (“calm fintech”, “neon skate shop”) and get a palette back via DeepSeek. Bring your own API key — it never leaves your session.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Do I need an account?
No. There’s no sign-up, no login, no email capture. Open the studio and start. Your palettes are stored in your own browser (with your permission), and Pro is unlocked with a license key or gift code.
Where is my data stored?
On your device, in your browser’s local storage — and only if you say yes to the storage prompt. Nothing is uploaded to us. There are no analytics, no cookies, and no trackers on this site.
What exactly does Pro unlock?
SCSS, Tailwind config, W3C design-token JSON, and SVG palette card exports (plus file downloads for each), and an unlimited palette library. The free plan keeps the full editor, WCAG tools, CSS export, share links, and 3 saved palettes.
Is it really one payment?
Yes. $29 once, yours forever, including future updates. There’s nothing to cancel because there’s no subscription.
How does the AI feature work?
You paste your own DeepSeek API key, describe a mood, and the AI returns a five-token palette that the studio validates and applies. Your key is kept only for the current session and is sent only to DeepSeek — never to us. The AI is optional; everything else works without it.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes — the studio is built mobile-first and works in any modern browser on iOS, Android, tablets, and desktops. Some extras (like the screen color picker) appear only on browsers that support them.
