How a palette gets loud

Hue, scored — never rolled

Candidate hues are scored against a memory of recent picks and weighted by mood; the wheel gets covered, never camped.

Chroma at the cusp

Saturation is set against the sRGB gamut ceiling for each lightness — loud lands loud, Pastel stays paper-soft, nothing clips.

Depth on purpose

A per-run overshoot past the floor puts accents at genuinely different depths. Rich or electric, always readable.

Tested like software, because it is

3,364 engine checks and 111 assertions run before a build ships, plus a browser suite that drives the product like a person — moods, harmonies, blends, exports, share URLs, licensing, the tour, both themes, and a 390px phone.

Seeds reproduce

A seeded generation returns the same palette every time, on any machine.

Blends stay honest

Feed it two hexes and the result is validated against the same floors as everything else.

FAQ

Straight answers

Do I need an account?

No. The studio opens and works without a login, and the free plan has no signup either.

Where does my work live?

In your browser's storage, after you accept the consent banner. Decline it and the studio keeps working — it only stops remembering between visits.

What does Pro add?

SCSS, Tailwind, W3C JSON, and SVG exports, an unlimited library, gift codes, and future updates. The designer itself is identical on both plans.

Is it one payment?

$29 once, licensed per person. Keys are redeemed in the studio and verified without any server.

How does the AI option work?

It is optional. You paste your own DeepSeek key, it lives only in that session, and the engine validates whatever comes back before it touches your palette.

Does it work on a phone?

It does. The studio is drawn for 390px and up, with 44px touch areas and both themes.
