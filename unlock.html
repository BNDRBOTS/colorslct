Already have a key? Activate it

Your key is on your Gumroad receipt, in your purchase email — or in a gift link someone sent you. Paste the whole thing.

What happens right after I pay?+

Gumroad shows your license key on the receipt screen immediately — paste it above and you’re done. Card purchases get their key by email. Either way, activation is one paste, no account.

Lost your key?+

Gumroad keys live in your Gumroad library forever. For card purchases, reply to your receipt email and a fresh key will be sent to you.

Refunds?+

If ColorSLCT isn’t for you, ask within 14 days of purchase and you’ll get your money back. Refunded keys stop working.

Which devices does one license cover?+

Pro covers you, on the devices you personally use. Studio covers up to five people. It’s an honor-system license backed by signed keys — we’d rather trust you than surveil you.
